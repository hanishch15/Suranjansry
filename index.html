02the apology

Dear Buggu🫀,

I’m sorry for the hurt I caused.

I’ve been thinking about what happened, and I want to be clear: I was wrong to let my concern become pressure instead of listening to what you needed. I can see how my words and choices hurt you, and I’m sorry I made you carry that.

I don’t want to soften it with excuses or ask you to look past it. I should have listened more carefully, considered how it would land, and treated your feelings with the respect they deserved.

You didn’t deserve to be hurt by someone who cares about you. I’m truly sorry.

03what I understand

Dear Buggu🫀,

I’ve tried to listen, even in the quiet.

I’m beginning to understand that the impact of what I did matters more than what I meant. I may not see every part of how it felt for you, but I believe you, and I’m not going to argue with your experience.

I’m sorry for pushing my worry about your health and plans instead of asking what kind of support you wanted. I imagine it may have left you feeling pressured or unheard, like I didn’t trust your independence. You deserved patience and honesty from me, not more reasons to explain why it hurt.

I’m working on understanding the choices behind it, so my apology can become changed behavior—not just a sentence I hope will make this easier.

04no conditions

Dear Buggu🫀,

You don’t owe me a response.

I want to say this without making it your job to reassure me: you do not have to forgive me, reply to this, or decide anything before you’re ready. I understand if you need distance, and I will respect that.

This apology isn’t a request for another chance. It’s an acknowledgment that I hurt you, and I’m responsible for that. I’ll keep doing the work because it’s mine to do, not because I expect something in return.

If you ever want to tell me more about what this was like for you, I will listen. If you don’t, I will respect that too.

05what comes next

Dear Buggu🫀,

I want to do better, quietly and consistently.

I know that trust doesn’t come back because I say the right thing. If it can be rebuilt, it will be through time, honesty, and the small choices I make from here on.

I’m going to ask what kind of support you want, listen to your answer, and respect it—even when I’m worried. I’ll keep listening, even when it’s uncomfortable, and I’ll take responsibility without making you remind me.

You matter to me. That is not a reason you should have to forgive me; it is a reason I should have treated you with more care in the first place.

POEM / 01

The space between

There is a space between
the words I wish I’d chosen
and the ones I let fall.

I have stood there since—
not asking you to cross it,
only learning how I built it.

Some bridges are not mine
to ask for.
Some lessons are mine
to keep.

POEM / 02

Not an answer

My sorry is not a key.
It cannot turn the day around,
or ask the lock to open.

It is a small, plain thing:
a hand set down,
no longer pointing anywhere.

A promise kept in the quiet
where no one is watching—
where change begins, or doesn’t.
